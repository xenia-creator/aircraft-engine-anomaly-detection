How It Works
A walkthrough of the anomaly detection pipeline
Step 1 of 10

Raw Sensor Data

21 sensors on each engine record temperature, pressure, and speed readings once per flight cycle — a continuous multivariate stream from takeoff to end of life.

Step 2 of 10

Feature Selection

7 sensors never move — they're dropped. The remaining 14 sensors carry real variance and define the feature space the scaler and model are fit on.

Step 3 of 10

Healthy Data Extraction

For each training engine, only the first 30% of its life is assumed healthy. That slice is what the autoencoder learns "normal" from.

Step 4 of 10

Autoencoder Training

A 14→8→4→8→14 network (ReLU encoder, Sigmoid output) squeezes each healthy reading through a 4-unit bottleneck and learns to reconstruct it on the way back out.

Step 5 of 10

Anomaly Scoring

Feed any reading back through the trained network and measure the gap between input and reconstruction. Healthy data reconstructs cleanly; degraded data doesn't — the error spikes.

Step 6 of 10

Dual Threshold Alerts

Two thresholds fire off the same error curve. Warning (2.8σ) is early enough to schedule an inspection; Critical (3.5σ) means ground the aircraft.

Step 7 of 10

Multi-Engine Validation

Run the same scoring across all 100 training engines and the pattern holds: flat and low while healthy, then a rise into failure — regardless of how long the engine lasted.

Step 8 of 10

Threshold Optimization

Sweeping k from 2.0σ to 3.0σ in 0.1 increments across all 100 engines picked out 2.8σ (105 cycles early warning, under 1 false alarm per engine) and 3.5σ (80 cycles early, near-zero false alarms).

Step 9 of 10

Model Comparison

Autoencoder reconstruction error gives a smooth, continuous signal (105 cycles of warning). Isolation Forest is noisier (72 cycles). DBSCAN only produces unstable binary in/out-of-cluster flips.

Step 10 of 10

Unseen Data Validation

Validated against 100 completely unseen test engines: reconstruction error tracks remaining life inversely — near-failure engines run hot, healthy ones stay under the thresholds.
